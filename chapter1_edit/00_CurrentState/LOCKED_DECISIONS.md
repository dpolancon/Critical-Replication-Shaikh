# Locked Decisions — Chapter 1 Editing System

These decisions should be preserved unless explicitly reopened.

## Econometric and interpretation locks

1. Shaikh’s baseline is approximately recoverable, not exactly reproduced.
2. The reconstructed coefficient is not assumed to be structurally self-sufficient.
3. The long-run output-capital coefficient may be interpreted as a candidate transformation elasticity only after the theoretical mapping is stated.
4. Statistical retention is not the same as economic admissibility.
5. Estimates such as `\hat{\theta}=1.19` may be statistically retained but economically rejected as capacity benchmarks.
6. Bivariate output-capital VECM systems do not pass the retained system-admissibility gates.
7. Restricted trivariate survival occurs under retained criteria when logged exploitation and historical shock controls enter the long-run system.
8. Historical dummies are shock controls, not proof of full causal mechanisms.
9. Military spending, household credit, and autonomous demand components are future research unless directly estimated.
10. Shaikh’s method is narrowed, not discarded.

## Process locks

1. Controlled-change version folders remain the manuscript-change mechanism until the user explicitly enters commit mode.
2. Do not stage, commit, or push as part of editing-system design.
3. Do not treat “Pass 7 ready” as “repo commit ready.”
4. Do not run another pass merely because a previous pass ended.
5. Human genre judgment can override an audit-only verdict.
