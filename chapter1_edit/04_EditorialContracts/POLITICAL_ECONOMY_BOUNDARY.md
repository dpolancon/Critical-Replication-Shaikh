# Political-Economy Boundary

## Principle

Political-economy interpretation must be earned by the empirical result.

## Allowed

The chapter may argue that the output-capital relation is not well interpreted as an insulated technical parameter once distribution and historical shock controls are needed for retained system survival.

The chapter may connect below-unity economically admissible estimates to a bounded overaccumulation interpretation.

The chapter may state that capacity-utilization measurement has political-economy content because the constructed utilization series depends on a coefficient conditioned by distributional and historical variables.

## Not allowed unless estimated

The chapter should not claim to estimate the causal mechanism of class conflict, the causal role of military spending, the causal role of household credit, a complete autonomous-demand stabilization mechanism, or a fully dynamic `\theta_t`.

## Tone

State political-economy stakes clearly but avoid turning diagnostic controls into causal proof.
