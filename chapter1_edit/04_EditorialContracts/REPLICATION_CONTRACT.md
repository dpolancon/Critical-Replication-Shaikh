# Replication Contract

## Replication first

The chapter must reconstruct Shaikh’s measure before criticizing it.

## Narrow target

The replication focuses on the coefficient used to draw the productive-capacity path and the utilization series that inherits from it.

## Required sequence

1. Identify Shaikh’s published or implied benchmark.
2. Reconstruct the closest recoverable version.
3. State what is recovered and what diverges.
4. Explain why the divergence matters for the constructed utilization series.
5. Test whether the coefficient is robust across defensible specification choices.
6. Move from single-equation checks to system-level checks.
7. State political-economy implications only within the evidence.

## Forbidden move

Do not begin by rejecting Shaikh’s framework wholesale.

## Preferred conclusion

Shaikh’s method is narrowed, not discarded.
