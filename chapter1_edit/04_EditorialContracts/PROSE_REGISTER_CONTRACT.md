# Prose Register Contract

## Governing rule

In manuscript prose, name the empirical item directly.

Do not call it an object if it is a coefficient, estimate, series, measure, benchmark, specification, test, relation, table, model, or data construction.

## Internal vs manuscript vocabulary

Internal ledgers may use: object, gate, lock, target, pass, audit, naturalization, system, candidate, protocol.

The manuscript should mostly use: coefficient, estimate, series, measure, benchmark, specification, relation, test, model, table, figure, data construction, empirical design.

## Examples

Avoid:

> The empirical object is the coefficient that generates the capacity path.

Prefer:

> The key estimate is the long-run output-capital coefficient used to draw the capacity path.

Avoid:

> Stage S2 tests whether the object remains system-admissible.

Prefer:

> Stage S2 asks whether the output-capital relation survives in a joint VECM system.

Avoid:

> The interpretive move reads the coefficient as a candidate transformation elasticity.

Prefer:

> I interpret the coefficient as an estimate of how capital accumulation translates into productive capacity.

## Scope

This contract governs manuscript prose, especially the abstract, introduction, Section 2 transitions, Section 3 opening, Section 4 opening, and conclusion.
