# Genre Contract — Applied Econometrics / Political Economy Critical Replication

## Target genre

This chapter should read as an applied econometrics political-economy critical replication.

## Core genre promise

The chapter reconstructs Shaikh’s capacity-utilization measure, identifies the coefficient that determines the productive-capacity path, tests the robustness of that coefficient, and states the political-economy implication within the limits of the evidence.

## Opening logic

The introduction should move through:

1. Why Shaikh’s measure matters.
2. What part of the measure must be replicated.
3. What can be approximately recovered.
4. Why the coefficient matters for the constructed utilization series.
5. How the ARDL and VECM tests evaluate robustness.
6. What the evidence supports and does not support.

## Style requirements

The prose should name empirical items directly, keep replication evidence before theoretical escalation, state claims in ordinary applied-econometrics language, reserve political-economy interpretation for moments where results have earned it, and avoid sounding like an editing ledger.

## What the genre does not need

The opening does not need heavy language about objects, gates, interpretive moves, ontology, or self-sufficiency before the reader understands the replication problem.
