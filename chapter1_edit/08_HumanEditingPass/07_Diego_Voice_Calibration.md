# Diego Voice Calibration

The goal is not to blend Ash, IZA, Basu, HAP, and old Diego into a single synthetic voice.

The goal is to recover the sequence that already exists in your writing.

## The sequence

1. Start from a real contradiction, not from a literature gap.
2. Show why the conventional empirical object is insufficient.
3. Build or adapt a method that makes the object observable.
4. Interpret the result as a social or historical mechanism, not as a table-only finding.

## Applied-econometrician layer

Source calibration: the Spanish paper on female labor-force participation and intergenerational mobility.

Use it for:

- defining the empirical object;
- explaining why existing methods are insufficient;
- stating the model;
- reporting the coefficient or test;
- interpreting the result with caution;
- saying what the result does not imply.

## Political-economy layer

Source calibration: the profit-rate paper on Chile.

Use it for:

- accumulation regimes;
- profitability and utilization;
- decomposition as political-economy method;
- measured variables as traces of social mechanisms;
- historically contingent distributional conflict.

## Do not blend the layers inside every sentence

Econometric paragraphs should report and discipline.

Political-economy paragraphs should interpret and raise the stakes.

The chapter should become applied econometrics inside political economy, not applied econometrics plus political economy.

## Anti-AI smoothing rule

Remove generic polish, not authorial texture.

Preserve:

- the contradiction-first cadence;
- the measurement-bottleneck logic;
- the methodological bridge;
- the move from empirical result to historical/social mechanism.

Delete or reduce:

- “underscores”;
- “pivotal”;
- “complex landscape”;
- “delves into”;
- repeated “this demonstrates that”;
- abstract claims that are not anchored in a table, figure, definition, or historical mechanism.
