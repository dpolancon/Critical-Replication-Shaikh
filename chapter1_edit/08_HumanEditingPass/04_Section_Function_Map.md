# Section Function Map

This note replaces a multi-pass editing system with a simpler question: what job is each section doing?

## Section 1 — Introduction

Job: tell the reader what is being replicated, why the coefficient matters, and what the chapter finds.

Failure mode: summarizing the full technical architecture too early.

Human edit: subtract detail; preserve the problem.

## Section 2 — Historical trace of capacity measurement

Job: explain why capacity utilization requires a constructed productive ceiling.

Failure mode: becoming a general history of Fordism, output-gap governance, central banking, neoliberalism, and inequality.

Human edit: keep history only when it clarifies the measurement problem.

## Section 3 — Conceptual framework

Job: turn Shaikh’s long-run coefficient into theta and explain balanced versus unbalanced capacity formation.

Failure mode: overloading notation and making the conceptual hinge harder than the empirical results.

Human edit: define terms once, slow down, make theta’s meaning unmistakable.

## Section 4.1–4.3 — Empirical design

Job: define what reproduction, sensitivity, and system testing mean.

Failure mode: presenting the method as an architecture before the reader knows the question.

Human edit: state S0, S1, and S2 as plain questions.

## Section 4.4 — S0

Job: show approximate reconstruction of Shaikh’s baseline.

Failure mode: treating discrepancy as indictment.

Human edit: use HAP-style fairness: target, reconstruction, discrepancy, implication.

## Section 4.5 — S1

Job: show that theta is disciplined but non-unique in the ARDL space.

Failure mode: drowning the claim in grid mechanics and criterion terminology.

Human edit: make the result legible before explaining the grid.

## Section 4.6 — S2

Job: test whether the output-capital relation survives as a joint system and whether distribution conditions that survival.

Failure mode: overclaiming “class-struggle parameter” or “severe omitted variable bias.”

Human edit: let the VECM counts carry the force. Then interpret.

## Section 4.7 — Cross-stage synthesis

Job: connect S0, S1, and S2 into one argument.

Failure mode: repeating all previous findings with more abstract wording.

Human edit: answer three questions: what reproduced, what varied, what stabilized.

## Section 5 — Conclusion

Job: state what the chapter establishes and what remains open.

Failure mode: importing future dissertation claims as if they were estimated in Chapter 1.

Human edit: separate findings, implications, and future research.
