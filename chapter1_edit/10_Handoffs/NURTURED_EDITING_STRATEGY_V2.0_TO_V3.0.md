## 🧭 1. Core Philosophy: Edit Against an Argument, Not a Dashboard

The new strategy shifts from a mechanical, automated "layer/pass" sequence to a human-guided editing loop. Rather than checking boxes on a dashboard, every rewrite must be judged against a single, clear intellectual movement:

> **The Central Argument:** Anwar Shaikh's capacity-utilization index can be approximately reconstructed. However, the output-capital coefficient that makes the index possible is not a mere technical scaling parameter. Once interpreted as a transformation elasticity ($\theta$) between capital accumulation and productive capacity, its stability and meaning are shown to depend on specification choice, historical controls, and the distributional structure of the macroeconomic system.

### A. The UMass Political Economy DNA ([UMassDNA_humanizing.md](file:///c:/ReposGitHub/Critical-Replication-Shaikh/chapter1_edit/01_Artifacts/UMassDNA_humanizing.md))
* **Contextualized Empiricism:** Quantitative models must never be "sterile." They must incorporate variables representing power, class bargaining, and institutional history (union density, minimum wages, etc.).
* **Intellectual Humility & Nuance:** Replace absolute claims (e.g., *"This proves that..."*) with cautious, qualified statements (e.g., *"These findings are consistent with..."*). Acknowledge limitations and alternative interpretations.
* **RadEconSynth Workflow:** Integrate a three-step editing loop for each section:
  1. *Deep-Research Alignment Check* (aligning with UMass themes).
  2. *Structural and Argumentative Critique* (identifying logic and context gaps).
  3. *Humanized Rewrite* (executing the prose register changes).

### B. House-Style Stylometrics & Voice Calibration ([ai_authorship_stress_test_ash_calibration.md](file:///c:/ReposGitHub/Critical-Replication-Shaikh/chapter1_edit/01_Artifacts/ai_authorship_stress_test_ash_calibration.md))
* **Stress-Testing Register over AI Detection:** We reject simple AI detectors. Instead, we run a **Register & Over-Smoothing Stress Test** targeting:
  - *Sentence-Length Burstiness:* Breaking up uniform mid-length sentences.
  - *Paragraph Rhythm:* Restructuring repetitive paragraph shapes across sections.
  - *Connective Tics:* Purging transition words like *moreover*, *furthermore*, *it is important to note*, and *this suggests that*.
* **Diego's Voice Calibration:** Restoring authorial texture, contradiction-first cadence, and empirical specificity.

### C. The Human Editing Pass Briefs ([08_HumanEditingPass/](file:///c:/ReposGitHub/Critical-Replication-Shaikh/chapter1_edit/08_HumanEditingPass/))
* **The "No-Blend" Rule ([07_Diego_Voice_Calibration.md](file:///c:/ReposGitHub/Critical-Replication-Shaikh/chapter1_edit/08_HumanEditingPass/07_Diego_Voice_Calibration.md)):** Keep applied-econometric paragraphs dry and disciplined (focusing on coefficients, statistics, and tables). Keep political-economy paragraphs interpretive and high-stakes. Do **not** blend these layers inside every sentence.
* **The Section-by-Section Briefs:** Use [03_Abstract_Intro_Rebuild_Brief.md](file:///c:/ReposGitHub/Critical-Replication-Shaikh/chapter1_edit/08_HumanEditingPass/03_Abstract_Intro_Rebuild_Brief.md) for the Introduction and [08_Historiographical_PE_Layer.md](file:///c:/ReposGitHub/Critical-Replication-Shaikh/chapter1_edit/08_HumanEditingPass/08_Historiographical_PE_Layer.md) for Section 2 to keep editing bounded.

## 📚 3. Integrating the PDF Reference Library ([09_papers_PDF/](file:///c:/ReposGitHub/Critical-Replication-Shaikh/chapter1_edit/09_papers_PDF/))

The papers in this directory serve as our style, register, and jargon anchor. We will integrate them into our strategy through two operational loops:

| Paper | Critical Concept | Role in Our Calibration & Cross-Referencing |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`HAP2014.pdf`** (Herndon, Ash, Pollin) | Critical Replication Order of Operations | **Primary Tone Anchor:** Model the exact sequence of a replication critique (State original claim ➔ Reproduce ➔ Identify source of divergence ➔ Test alternatives ➔ State stakes). |
| **`BasuChenOhVARclassStruggle.pdf`** | Empirics of Class Conflict & Reserve Army | **Conceptual Bridge:** Ground the Section 2.2 transition to labor bargaining and reserve army dynamics in rigorous VAR/time-series language rather than narrative assertions. |
| **`pollin-1998...pdf`** | Reserve Army & Unemployment | **Contextual Grounding:** Calibrate the description of shop-floor conflict, wage squeeze, and capital reactions in Section 2.2. |
| **`AshBasuDube2017.pdf`** | Collaborative Empirical Rigor | **Style Reference:** Guide the integration of policy-relevant arguments and large-scale dataset descriptions without sounding over-polished. |

### Operational Loops for the PDF Library:
1. **The Exemplar Extraction Loop:** Before writing or editing a specific manuscript section, the agent will extract 2–3 representative paragraphs from the relevant reference PDF (e.g., extracting from the methodology section of `HAP2014.pdf` before editing Section 4 of `main.tex`). These paragraphs are injected as few-shot style examples to enforce the correct sentence length and vocabulary register.
2. **The Vocabulary Cross-Referencing Loop (Strictly Calibrated to Ash):** During the self-audit step of the rewrite, the agent must cross-reference the drafted vocabulary and syntax specifically against the text of the two Michael Ash papers: **`AshBasuDube2017.pdf`** and **`HAP2014.pdf`**. 
   - *Active Check:* If the draft uses high-level abstractions or metadata terms that do not appear in these two reference papers (e.g., "empirical architecture", "system-admissibility gates"), they will be flagged for replacement with the standard terms used by Ash (e.g., "empirical design", "system specification", "cointegration checks").
   - *Style Validation:* Ensure sentence length distributions and transition choices mirror Ash's collaborative empirical and critical-replication register.

## 🔄 4. Bounded Section-by-Section Workflow (v2.0 to v3.0)

We will execute the rewrite starting from the clean **Version 2.0** manuscript ([02_Versions/WP_CriticalReplication_2.0/main.tex](file:///c:/ReposGitHub/Critical-Replication-Shaikh/chapter1_edit/02_Versions/WP_CriticalReplication_2.0/main.tex)) using the following sequence:

### Step 1: Initialize Bounded Experiment (e.g., `EXP_01`)
Create the experiment directory and write the intake self-audit to lock paragraph eligibility. 
* *Constraint:* No edits are allowed outside the authorized paragraph boundaries of the current section.

### Step 2: Extract Stylistic Exemplars
Read the corresponding human brief (e.g., `03_Abstract_Intro_Rebuild_Brief.md`) and extract 2–3 paragraphs from the target PDF (e.g., `HAP2014.pdf` for the intro) to load into the writing agent's active memory.

### Step 3: Execute the "RadEconSynth" Loop
1. **Deep-Research Check:** Verify that the section's argument aligns with the UMass PE DNA (distributional conflict, contextualized empiricism).
2. **Argumentative Critique:** Audit the draft's logic. Ensure that technical arguments remain dry and distinct from political-economy interpretations (the **No-Blend Rule**).
3. **Draft the Rewrite:** Generate the draft rewrite, preserving all mathematical symbols and econometric findings exactly as locked.

### Step 4: Run Automated Prose & Jargon Gates
1. Run [check_jargon_leakage.py](file:///c:/ReposGitHub/academic-research-skills/scripts/check_jargon_leakage.py) to scan for blocklist terms.
2. Run the **Register & Over-Smoothing Stress Test** to check sentence-length variance and transition tics.
3. If violations are found, perform targeted edits. Repeat until the gates report `SUCCESS`.

### Step 5: Socratic Committee Review
Submit the clean draft to `/PE-phd-committee` and `/advisor-reviewer` to ensure the econometric claims and heterodox arguments are structurally robust.

### Step 6: Promote to Candidate Version
Once approved, save the section in the candidate directory, and proceed to the next experiment folder (e.g., `EXP_02` for Section 3). When all sections are complete, compile the final **v3.0** manuscript.
