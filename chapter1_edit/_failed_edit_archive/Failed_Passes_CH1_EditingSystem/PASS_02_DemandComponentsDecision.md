# PASS 02 Demand Components Decision

## Audit

Pass 1 removed the conclusion's direct finding-language reference to "military spending and consumer debt expansion." Pass 2 checked whether these components are estimated in Chapter 1.

## Decision

Military spending, household credit, consumer debt, and other autonomous demand components are not estimated in the current Chapter 1 empirical design. They cannot appear as empirical findings.

They may appear only as theoretical implications or future research, with explicit wording that they are outside the present chapter's empirical design.

## Manuscript Revision

The conclusion now states:

> The chapter does not estimate the demand components that may bound this tendency. A fuller account would need to examine whether military spending, household credit, or other autonomous demand components stabilize the capacity path across historical regimes.

This preserves the political-economy implication without converting unestimated variables into results.
