# READING_AUDIT_01 Argument-Flow Check

This check follows the reader-facing sequence from problem statement to bounded conclusion. No manuscript changes were made.

| Step | Where it appears | Clear to reader? | Too early / too late? | Comment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Latent capacity-utilization object | Abstract, Introduction, Section 2 opening | Yes | No | The chapter begins from the unobserved productive ceiling and keeps the measurement problem visible. |
| Shaikh's solution | Abstract, Introduction, Section 2.4 transition into Section 3 | Yes | No | The accounting-based capacity path is introduced before the critique, which helps the reader understand what is being replicated. |

| Bounded econometric finding | Abstract, Introduction, Section 4.7, Conclusion | Yes | No | The finding remains calibrated: recoverable baseline, non-unique single-equation alternatives, and restricted system survival under conditioning. |

| Future-research boundary | Conclusion | Yes | No | Demand components are correctly treated as future research scope rather than established findings. |

Flow verdict: the argument is sequenced coherently for a dissertation reader. The chapter moves from measurement problem, to historical context, to conceptual interpretation, to staged econometric evidence, to a bounded political-economy implication.
