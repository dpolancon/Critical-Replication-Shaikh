## Scope

## Before

and:

## After

The opening now does three narrower tasks:

1. Restates the object tested in Section 4: the output--capital relation itself, not only the utilization proxy.
2. Introduces S0, S1, and S2 once, while preserving the statistical/economic admissibility distinction.
3. States the calibrated boundary-condition result: the bivariate relation fails the retained system-admissibility gates and the trivariate system survives under retained criteria with distributional conditioning.

Representative revised text:

> The empirical question is whether the relation itself, not only the utilization proxy derived from it, remains stable across admissible specifications and joint system estimation.

> The objective is therefore not to discard Shaikh's portability strategy, but to locate its boundary conditions.
