# What Not To Repeat

- Do not run another pass merely because the prior pass ended.
- Do not overfit HAP as visible prose structure.
- Do not reward schematic completeness as genre success.
- Do not use internal editing vocabulary in manuscript prose.
- Do not let consistency audits settle genre questions.
- Do not use “humanization” as a vague instruction.

The problem is not generic AI polish. The problem is genre fit, register, pacing, and concrete empirical naming.
