# Delta 2.0 vs 2.7 — Initial Assessment

## Provisional diagnosis

Version 2.7 is cleaner, safer, and more internally consistent than 2.0. It does not yet strongly contrast with 2.0 as a reader-facing critical replication paper.

## Main improvements from 2.0 to 2.7

- overclaims were softened;
- “proof” language was reduced;
- Shaikh’s method is narrowed rather than discarded;
- statistical retention and economic admissibility are separated;
- the conclusion is better bounded;
- future-research boundaries are clearer.

## Remaining genre problem

The manuscript still relies too heavily on system-like abstractions and editorial scaffolding: “measurement problem,” “replication target,” “empirical object,” “candidate transformation elasticity,” “interpretive move,” “system-admissibility gates,” “retained criteria,” and “bounded implications.”

These terms are not all wrong, but they should not form the spine of the introduction or early conceptual exposition.

## Strategic conclusion

2.7 sanitized 2.0. It did not yet fully reorganize the chapter around a sharper applied-econometrics political-economy critical replication contribution.
