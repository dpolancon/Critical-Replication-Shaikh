# Genre Gap Audit — Seed

## Target genre

Applied econometrics / political economy critical replication.

## What the chapter should sound like

The chapter should sound like a paper that reconstructs a published measure, identifies the coefficient that matters for that measure, tests the robustness of that coefficient, and then states the political-economy implication only after the empirical evidence has earned it.

## What the chapter should not sound like

The chapter should not sound like an editing protocol, an ontology of empirical objects, a pass report, a methods memo, a synthetic abstract expanded into paragraphs, or a literature-theory manifesto before the replication problem is concrete.

## Main genre gap

The current introduction is internally coherent but too system-like. It often describes the chapter’s “objects,” “targets,” “moves,” and “gates” instead of directly naming the estimate, coefficient, series, benchmark, specification, and tests.

## Genre priority

The first genre repair should target the introduction, then Section 3 opening, then the Section 2.2 political-economy transition if necessary.
